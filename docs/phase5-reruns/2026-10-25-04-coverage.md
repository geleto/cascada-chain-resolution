The graph oracle's two identity maps are a useful independent check: they detect both split aliases and accidentally merged identities, and check prototypes, holes, length, property order and exact leaves. Those checks still establish only the graphs the harness constructs. A strong oracle cannot cover an absent operation, lifetime event or topology.

## Coverage added and remaining gaps

The run 04 work, lifetime and delivery reports record new cases, their actual outcomes and limits. A passing cell means that concrete combination was checked. A source trace, an old passing result, a new successful execution and a reproduced failure remain separate kinds of evidence.

This focused pass does not rerun every B01–B22 source route, all eight large generated families, the full conflict/publication matrices, or the full safeguard registry. It does not claim exhaustive Proxy failure-site coverage, arbitrary graph depth, every Array method under every lifetime state, or a bound on total runtime work from selected counters. No general defect-free conclusion follows from this review.
