## Producers and consumers checked separately

The public surface was re-enumerated from `src/index.js`: import/result import; lookup/expression/export/queries; assignment/deletion/repair; calls/entry; Chain/context construction; declarations; and guarded composition/Error transport exports. Receiving callers use `receiveValue` in import, argument preparation and placement input preparation; Chain/context and assignment publish through `transferPlacement`/`prepareInputPlacement`.

`importReadyMethodResult` is a deliberate ready-only `prepareInput` bypass. Both managed and external call completion immediately acquire output protection through `retainOutput`, so a prepared retired root is restored through existing lease acquisition before it escapes. Its newly pending descendants use the same staged producer policy. Detached external snapshot assembly instead admits private shells and initializes final topology; it must not import mutable raw source storage in place. `captureExternalValue` clears source maps and ends external access after its ready-only capture, before canonical assembly can wait.

All admission/preparation writers were enumerated with `rg`. Internal root holders initialize their own empty placement map; Array view/remap construction and path-COW copies inherit known categories and publish through `PlacementConstruction`; property publication consumes a prepared placement and validates the destination value. Refcount indexing does not acquire an independent receiving role. These bypasses do not grant fresh host data preparation merely from metadata existence. The detailed Array identity/backing algorithms and their full method catalogue are assigned to the sibling audit.

The source-lifetime review followed failed-inspection maps, staging entries, exact receiver policy, receiver/argument captures, query source captures and external routes through success/failure and late delivery. Their separate last-use rules remain necessary; combining them behind one invocation completion would retain arguments and native reservations unnecessarily, while releasing all at first poison would omit required processing. No simplification was proposed without a replacement for those invariants.
